# Sales-Intelligence-And-Performance
The Sales Intelligence Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution developed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, delivery status, and salesperson productivity. 

📌 Project Overview

The Sales Intelligence Performance Dashboard is an interactive Power BI solution developed to analyze sales performance, revenue trends, product performance, delivery status, and salesperson productivity. The dashboard transforms raw sales order data into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders monitor KPIs and make data-driven decisions.

The report is designed with multiple interactive pages featuring drill-through navigation, custom tooltips, bookmarks, slicers, and dynamic visualizations for enhanced user experience.

🎯 Business Problem

Organizations generate thousands of sales transactions every month, making it difficult to monitor performance manually. Management requires a centralized dashboard to answer key business questions such as:

Which products generate the highest revenue?
Which regions contribute the most sales?
How efficient is the delivery process?
Which sales representatives perform best?
What are the monthly sales trends?
Where can the business improve operational efficiency?

This dashboard addresses these challenges through interactive analytics and visualization.

📈 Business Insights

💰 Revenue Performance

Electronics products contribute a significant share of total revenue.
Product-level analysis identifies the highest revenue-generating products for inventory planning.
Revenue distribution helps prioritize profitable product categories.

🌍 Regional Analysis

Chennai generates the highest revenue among all cities.
City-wise performance highlights potential markets for future business expansion.

🚚 Delivery Performance

Most orders are delivered on time, indicating a healthy fulfillment process.
Delayed orders can be monitored to improve logistics efficiency.

👨‍💼 Sales Representative Analysis

Salesperson performance comparison identifies top contributors.
Low-performing representatives can receive additional training and support.

📦 Product Performance

Revenue by Product highlights customer purchasing preferences.
High-performing products can be prioritized for inventory optimization and promotional campaigns.

📊 Trend Analysis
Monthly sales trends help identify seasonal demand patterns.
Businesses can plan marketing campaigns based on revenue fluctuations.
